Operating method

How I diagnose operating failure.

Most persistent execution problems are not caused by incapable people. They form when evidence is inaccessible, decision rights are unclear, or decisions do not survive time and scale.

Three conditions
Truth
Whether narrative aligns with evidence.

Can leaders, operators, and customers see the same reality? When narrative and evidence separate, organizations begin solving the wrong problem.

Authority
Whether decision rights match accountability.

Can the person held accountable actually decide? Responsibility without authority produces delay, workarounds, and permanent escalation.

Continuity
Whether decisions, rationale, and learning persist over time.

Do decisions and reasoning survive turnover, reorganization, and scale? When organizations lose memory, the same issues return under new names.

Application

Where I use it.

I use this lens in customer operations, client delivery, partner governance, enterprise transformation, and AI deployment. The objective is not to produce another framework. It is to identify the structural condition preventing execution and correct it.
