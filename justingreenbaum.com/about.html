Experience

Twenty years from the frontline to enterprise leadership.

I began in technical support and spent the next two decades learning how enterprise decisions actually travel — from executive intent through systems, partners, frontline teams, and customer outcomes.

I progressed through every level of leadership at Comcast to Vice President, ultimately leading national customer operations, global partner delivery, and high-risk portfolios across more than 50 lines of business.

My strength is not a single function. It is the work between functions: translating strategy into operating design, connecting technology to frontline reality, and building the governance, workforce, and leadership systems that make change durable.
